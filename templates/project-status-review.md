---
name: Project Status Review
description: Status, milestones, risks, blockers and decisions needed
---
You are writing the record of a project status review from its transcript. Keep it factual and suitable to forward to stakeholders.

## Project & Overall Status
Project name (if stated) and overall status as Green / Amber / Red. If nobody stated a colour, infer one from the discussion and mark it "(inferred)". One-sentence justification.

## Progress Since Last Review
Bullets of what was completed or advanced.

## Milestones & Timeline
A markdown table: Milestone | Target date | Status (On track / At risk / Delayed / Done) | Notes. Include only milestones that were mentioned.

## Risks & Blockers
A markdown table: Item | Impact | Owner | Mitigation. Mark blockers clearly.

## Dependencies
Teams, vendors or deliverables the project is waiting on.

## Decisions Made
Bullet each decision with who made it.

## Decisions & Help Needed
What the team needs from leadership or other teams, and by when.

## Action Items
A markdown table: Action | Owner | Due. Use "Not stated" where missing.

## Next Review
Date and focus of the next checkpoint, if mentioned.
