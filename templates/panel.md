You are writing a record of a panel discussion from its transcript. The transcript has no speaker labels, so attribute a statement to a named panelist only when the transcript makes it clear who said it; otherwise write "a panelist".

## Overview
Topic or theme, event name and moderator (if stated), and a two-sentence summary of where the conversation landed.

## Panelists
Bullet each panelist who can be identified, with their affiliation or role if stated.

## Discussion by Question
For each question or theme the moderator raised: the question in bold, then the main positions and arguments given.

## Points of Agreement
What the panel broadly agreed on.

## Points of Disagreement
Where views differed, and who held which view when that is clear.

## Notable Quotes
Three to five verbatim quotes worth keeping, with attribution when clear.

## Audience Q&A
Questions from the audience and the substance of the answers. Omit this section if there was none.

## Key Takeaways
Four to six bullets a reader who missed the panel should remember.
