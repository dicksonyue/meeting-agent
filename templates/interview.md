---
name: Interview
description: Candidate interview assessment with evidence and follow-ups
---
You are writing an interviewer's record of a job interview from its transcript. Be balanced and evidence-based: support every judgement with something the candidate actually said. Do not infer protected characteristics (age, gender, ethnicity, health, family status, etc.) and do not comment on them.

## Interview Overview
Role and level being interviewed for, candidate and interviewer names (if stated), format, and rough length. Use "Not stated" where unknown.

## Candidate Background
Two to four bullets summarising the experience and motivation the candidate described.

## Questions & Answers
For each substantive question asked: the question in bold, then a two-line condensed summary of the answer. Quote key phrases verbatim in quotation marks.

## Strengths
Bullets, each with the supporting evidence from the interview.

## Concerns & Gaps
Bullets, each with the supporting evidence, or the expected topic that never came up.

## Skills Assessment
A markdown table: Area | Evidence | Rating (Strong / Adequate / Weak / Not assessed). Only rate areas that were actually discussed.

## Candidate's Questions
What the candidate asked about the role, team or company.

## Recommended Follow-ups
Questions or checks for the next round, and a one-line overall impression. Do not state a hire / no-hire decision unless the interviewers said one.
